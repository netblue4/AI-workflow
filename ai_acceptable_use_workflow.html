Tool fitness evaluation new ›
ISG Team + AI Taskforce AI SR2AI SR4 Tier 2 / elevated risk

Before assessing the use case, evaluate whether the AI tool itself is fit for the intended domain. This is a prerequisite to the risk assessment — the tool's properties, not just the use case around it, must be formally assessed. Evaluation covers factual reliability, known bias characteristics, training data provenance and IP exposure, and vendor contractual protections.

Tool evaluation report: domain reliability, known bias profile, training data provenance
Vendor assessment: IP indemnity, data processing terms, contractual safeguards
Hallucination risk rating for the intended domain (e.g. legal, medical, financial)
Hard stop: If the tool cannot be evaluated or vendor terms are insufficient, the use case must be redesigned around a tool that can be assessed. The risk assessment in Step 7 is only valid if this evaluation is complete.

Phase 4 — Operational controls · CISO + ISG Team

12
Operational controls activation new ›
CISO + ISG Team AI SR1AI SR4AI SR8 Live operation

Before any user accesses the approved tool, the controls defined in Step 9 must be active and verified. This step closes the gap between approval-time assessment and live operation — it ensures system protections do not evaporate once the workflow ends. Controls are the organisation's responsibility, not the user's.

Output logging active and reviewed against logging standards
Human review checkpoints configured for high-stakes content (Tier 2)
Disclosure and labelling templates deployed and tested
Anomaly flagging or output monitoring mechanism in place
Access controls enforced: no Group entity email addresses, MFA required
Gate: Tool access must not be granted until all controls from the approved control schedule are confirmed active. This is verified by ISG, not self-certified by the requestor.
AI SR1 — Governance controlsAI SR4 — System-level verificationAI SR8 — Disclosure active
13
User obligations briefing new ›
ISG Team + Authorised Users AI SR4AI SR7 Live operation

Before access is granted, authorised users receive a use-case-specific briefing that tells them exactly what they are responsible for — and what the system is responsible for. The standard's AI SR4 list is replaced here with a narrower, genuinely achievable set of behavioural obligations. Users are not asked to verify things the system should prevent.

System responsibilities

  • Accuracy and hallucination risk evaluation (done at Step 6)
  • Bias detection and domain suitability (Step 6 + Step 10)
  • IP and copyright exposure (vendor terms, Step 6)
  • Output logging and anomaly flagging (Step 12)
  • Disclosure labelling on published content (Step 12)

User responsibilities

  • Not inputting data known to be personal, confidential, or classified
  • Flagging outputs that are clearly wrong or inconsistent with their professional knowledge
  • Not publishing or acting on output without the required human review step
  • Deleting chat history after each session as instructed
  • Reporting anomalous, harmful, or unexpected outputs to ISG
Key principle: Users are held to obligations they can actually discharge. Accuracy, bias, and IP verification are organisational controls — not individual responsibilities.
AI SR4 — Revised user obligationsAI SR7 — Awareness

Periodic and triggered review ›
ISG Team + SteerCo AI SR5AI SR7 All approved use cases

Periodic reviews confirm the business case remains valid, risk assessments are current, operational controls are still active and effective, and the tool fitness evaluation reflects any model updates from the vendor. Triggered reviews are initiated immediately following security incidents or significant changes to technology, regulation, or Group structure. Review outcomes feed back into training programmes and are reported to SteerCo.

Review schedule at risk-proportionate frequency
Triggered review mechanism active: security incidents, vendor model changes, regulatory updates
Review outcomes: updated tool evaluation, controls verification, SteerCo report
Model change trigger: If the vendor updates the underlying model, the tool fitness evaluation (Step 6) must be re-run before continued use is permitted. Model updates are not covered by the original authorisation.
Training feedback loop: Trends from reviews must be escalated to ISG for targeted training programmes under AI SR7.
